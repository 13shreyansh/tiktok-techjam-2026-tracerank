# Run93 report: seed-saturation audit stopped for submission freeze

Run93 predeclared a fixed twelve-seed equal within-user-rank consensus to test
whether the selected causal sequence-NFM had remaining initialization diversity.
The protocol required nine new members on each of three chronological shadows
before any official construction.

The campaign stopped at the user's submission freeze before completing its
first window. It contains eight counted attempts: one pre-model failure caused
by restricted Apple MPS detection and seven successful fixed members for seeds
2029 through 2035. The exact retry used the intended MPS device without changing
the model hypothesis. Total recorded subprocess time was `300.133684` seconds;
maximum RSS was `3,421,454,336` bytes.

Seeds 2036 and 2037 were not trained, no twelve-seed consensus was formed, and
no transfer or promotion gate was evaluated. Consequently Run93 has no candidate
score, no official build, and no final-test prediction. Its partial artifacts
remain ignored. The selected candidate is still the clean Run84 six-member
consensus at validation primary `0.605374519999571`.

No final-test outcomes were loaded, and nothing was submitted, uploaded, pushed,
or made public. Run93 closed solely to transition from experimentation to
submission preparation.
