# Strategic audit: Run91 separate explicit-engagement history

## Fresh-context diagnosis

Run90 confirms that another recency view of the same long-view-positive history
does not transfer. Earlier behavior experiments also do not answer the narrower
question here. Run7 put action bits into one shared history, so frequent and
rare actions competed inside the same sequence. Run86 used click as an
auxiliary target rather than as a separate candidate-conditioned interest
profile. Neither preserved the selected long-view path while isolating rare,
strong positive actions.

The workshop explicitly identified behavior history as useful ranking context,
and the training log contains likes, follows, comments, and forwards. Those
actions are observed only on earlier training impressions. They can therefore
define a second causal profile without using validation or final-test outcomes.

## Frozen hypothesis

Preserve the exact Run83 seed-2027 parent: last-20 chronological long-view
history, tag-enhanced dot attention, 16-dimensional embeddings, 128 hidden
units, neural FM term, and unchanged optimizer. Add one second last-20 history
containing only earlier impressions with a like, follow, comment, or forward.
The candidate attends to this channel independently; its profile, elementwise
candidate product, and absolute difference are appended to the parent input.

Click-only events and hate feedback do not enter this new channel. History
length, attention type, architecture, loss, seed, and weights are frozen before
scoring. No validation or final-test label contributes to either history.

## Third-person winning-goal check

This is an independent representation test, not a length, seed, weight, or
blend sweep. It directly tests a clue from the organizer workshop while keeping
the protected signal intact. Begin with paired seed 2027 on the early window.
Require at least `+0.0005` validation primary, nonnegative forward primary,
component losses no worse than `-0.0005`, and every fixed slice no worse than
`-0.001`. Failure closes the family immediately. Run84 remains protected.
