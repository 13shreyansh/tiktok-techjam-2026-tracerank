# Fresh strategic audit before Run93

## Current evidence

The protected clean Pure candidate remains Run84 at primary
`0.605374519999571`. Runs85–92 tested materially distinct history, auxiliary,
residual-ranking, aggregation, and attention ideas; none transferred through
their frozen opening gates. The latest three history-complexity variants all
hurt high-activity users. This is consistent evidence against another
near-duplicate history representation.

The remaining credible variance-reduction question is ensemble saturation.
Earlier exact artifacts show an official validation increase from the causal
three-seed user-rank consensus (`0.6052221668551088`) to the historical causal
six-seed consensus (`0.6055212246527164`). The latter cannot be submitted
because its old loader crossed the final-test outcome boundary, but the
development-score comparison still motivates a fresh label-blind test of
additional initialization diversity. Run84 supplies the clean six-member
anchor.

## Why not the apparent alternatives

- Organizer-FM plus sequence blending was already scored at fixed 0.1 and 0.2
  corrections; the old official FM loader is now correctly quarantined.
- More history pooling, negative/engagement channels, hard matching, GRU, and
  self-attention have recent direct transfer failures.
- Validation-only weighting, seed selection, or activity routing would reopen
  a high-dimensional micro-tuning surface with weak hidden-period evidence.
- Final-test outcomes remain prohibited and provide no decision signal.

## Decision

Run one fixed twelve-seed equal-rank saturation family. Demand positive mean
validation and forward transfer, two passing chronological windows, component
and slice safety, and a material official gain before promotion. Close on two
shadow failures or after the single official construction. The expected gain
is small, so the purpose is not to manufacture iterations; it is to test the
last demonstrated source of complementary error while preserving Run84.
