## Objective

Test the workshop clue that clicks, likes, follows, comments, forwards, negative
feedback, and prior viewing behavior should inform candidate ranking. Preserve
the selected target-aware video/tag history architecture and official
`long_view` target while adding action identity to strictly past events.

## Evaluation and promotion

- First test action embeddings on the same positive long-view history as the
  paired parent. This isolates richer feedback from changing history density.
- Only if justified, test a history containing any explicit behavior event,
  including `is_hate`, so negative feedback is represented.
- Require at least +0.001 early validation, no forward loss beyond 0.0005, and
  no material activity/date regression before multi-window or seed promotion.
- Require middle and late chronological windows plus official seeds 2026,
  2027, and 2028 before any final ensemble change.
- Keep public-test labels locked and preserve the exact Run 2 fallback.

## Limits and stopping

Count every attempt up to 50, stop within six hours, enforce a ten-minute
subprocess timeout, and write a fresh strategic review after each family or
eight attempts. Stop after the two predeclared behavior-sequence variants fail
the promotion rule. Do not submit, upload, push, contact organizers, use
credentials, change registration, or change repository visibility.
